[INTERNAL POLICY: DATABASE RETENTION SETTINGS]
Document ID: SEC-2026-RET01
Target System: Main Production Database Cluster
Current Retention Configuration:
- Transaction logging history is preserved natively for a standard duration of 90 days.
- Backup snapshots are securely archived across three isolated availability zones.
- Customer session cache tokens are set to clear after 24 hours of inactivity.
- Authorized jurisdiction for storage: Global Cloud Infrastructure.
